Welcome to **Friction Hunter** — an engineering blog dedicated to identifying, breaking down, and overcoming technical and cognitive friction in software engineering.

## What You'll Find Here

- **System Architecture & SRE:** Mental models for designing resilient, scalable distributed systems.
- **Cognitive Science & Learning:** Applying neuroscience, spaced repetition, and active recall to escape "tutorial hell".
- **Interactive Deep Dives:** Interactive visual tools, diagrams, and code models built to solidify complex technical concepts.
