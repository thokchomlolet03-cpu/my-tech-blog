## ⚡ Pedagogical Framework: The "Scaffold and Shatter" Engine

First principles build unshakeable mastery, but starting immediately at the bare metal introduces severe **intrinsic cognitive load**. Human working memory can only process 4 to 7 new items simultaneously.

To optimize comprehension without sacrificing engineering rigor, we use **The Destructive Analogy** (Scaffold & Shatter) framework:

1. **The Anchor (The Analogy)**: Maps complex concepts onto familiar mental schemas to bypass working memory limits.
2. **The Shatter (The Flaw)**: Intentionally breaks the analogy where it fails in distributed cloud environments, destroying false equivalence.
3. **The Bare Metal (The First Principle)**: Fills the gap with the physical, low-level mechanical truth.
