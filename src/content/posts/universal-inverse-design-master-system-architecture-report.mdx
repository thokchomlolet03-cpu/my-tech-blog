# 3. Hemisphere 2: Universal Inverse Design Engine (`uid_engine`)
**Root Path:** `src/uid_engine/`

The `uid_engine` takes the compiled causal requirements and executes the automated biophysical discovery loop.

### 3.1 Strict Knowledge Graph Schema (`graph/schema.py`)
The system ontology is strictly typed across 9 Node Types, 11 Edge Types, and 7 Evidence States:
*   **Node Types:** `MOLECULE`, `PROTEIN`, `GENE`, `PATHWAY`, `TISSUE`, `PAPER`, `DAMAGE_CLASS`, `MECHANISM`, `UNKNOWN`.
*   **Edge Types:** `CATALYZES`, `CROSSLINKS`, `CAUSES_DAMAGE`, `REPORTED_IN`, `REQUIRES`, `PART_OF`, `INHIBITS`, `ACTIVATES`, `DEGRADES`, `PRODUCES`, `BLOCKS`.
*   **Evidence Status Lifecycle:**
    ```
    UNKNOWN -> HYPOTHESIZED_IN_SILICO -> SYNTHESIS_ORDERED -> ASSAY_VALIDATED -> ANIMAL_MODEL_TESTED -> PROVEN
    ```

### 3.2 Negative Space Gap Detector (`analysis/gap_detector.py`)
Traverses causal trees (e.g. `glucosepane.yaml`) and verifies every requirement against the knowledge graph.
*   **O(1) Edge Type Indexing:** Pre-indexes graph edges by type (`_edge_type_index`) to prevent catastrophic `N * E` scans on large graphs.
*   **Gap Classification:** Identifies `MISSING_MECHANISM`, `UNPROVEN_HYPOTHESIS`, or `CROSS_DOMAIN_BLIND_SPOT`.
*   **Graph Metrics:** Computes `network_betweenness` and `redundancy_index` to quantify single-point failure bottlenecks in human understanding.

### 3.3 Layer 3 De Novo Design Spec Compiler (`analysis/design_spec.py`)
Converts epistemic gaps into machine-actionable 3D biophysical parameters:
*   **3D Ligand Embedding:** Employs RDKit `ETKDGv3` with MMFF94 force-field optimization to convert 1D SMILES (e.g., Glucosepane: `O=C(O)[C@@H](N)CCCCNC1=NC2=C(N1)CC(O)[C@H](NCC[C@@H](N)C(=O)O)CN2`) into 3D Cartesian conformers (`.sdf`).
*   **Target Pocket Constraints:** Sets pocket volume requirements (~1200 cubic Angstroms), active-site catalytic motifs, and prompts for RFdiffusion and ESM-3.

### 3.4 The 6-Gate In Silico Quality Control Sieve (`generative/screening.py`)
No candidate reaches wet-lab handoff without clearing all 6 gates:
1.  **Gate 1 (pLDDT Structural Confidence):** Mean pLDDT &ge; 80.0 evaluated via ESM-2/ESM-3.
2.  **Gate 2 (Self-Consistency Foldability):** scRMSD &le; 2.0 Angstroms computed via 3D Kabsch alignment (`Bio.PDB.Superimposer`) between designed backbone and predicted fold.
3.  **Gate 3 (Catalytic Pocket Integrity):** Verifies active-site spatial preservation (e.g. Serine Hydrolase triad `His54 - Asp112 - Ser198`).
4.  **Gate 4 (Biophysical Solubility & Stability):** BioPython ProtParam evaluation:
    *   GRAVY Score &le; 0.2 (Ensures water solubility; avoids aggregation).
    *   Instability Index &le; 50.0 (Ensures structural longevity in solution).
    *   Calculates Isoelectric Point (pI) and Net Charge at physiological pH 7.4.
5.  **Gate 5 (AutoDock Vina Thermodynamic Binding):** Binding Energy &Delta;G &le; -8.0 kcal/mol. Uses Meeko to generate PDBQT files with active rotatable bonds.
6.  **Gate 6 (Off-Target Counter-Screening vs Decoys):** Requires &ge; 100x binding selectivity over native human structural and plasma decoys:
    *   *Collagen Type I (&alpha;1)* (`P02452`)
    *   *Collagen Type III* (`P02461`)
    *   *Arterial Elastin* (`P15502`)
    *   *Human Serum Albumin (HSA)* (`P02768`)
    *   *Fibronectin* (`P02751`)
    *   *BCL-xL* (`Q07817`)

### 3.5 Industrial Gene Synthesis Handoff (`generative/synthesis_handoff.py`)
Transforms passing candidates into wet-lab ready DNA expression plasmids:
*   **Codon Optimization:** Biased for *E. coli* BL21(DE3) expression (Codon Adaptation Index CAI &ge; 0.88).
*   **GC-Content Window:** Enforced strictly between 45% &le; GC &le; 55%.
*   **Sequence Purges:** Homopolymers (&ge; 5 consecutive identical nucleotides) and internal restriction sites (`NdeI: CATATG`, `XhoI: CTCGAG`, `BamHI: GGATCC`, `EcoRI: GAATTC`, `BsaI: GGTCTC`) are silently mutated via synonymous codon replacement.
*   **Plasmid Assembly:** Inserts sequence into `pET-28a(+)` containing an N-terminal `6xHis-tag` and a cleavable `TEV protease` site (`ENLYFQ/G`).
*   **Deliverables:** Exports automated Twist Bioscience Batch Order CSVs and annotated GenBank (`.gb`) plasmid maps.
